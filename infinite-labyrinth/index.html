Maze · iPhone, iPad, Android · Free

Infinite Labyrinth

Find the way out. Beat the clock. Swipe, and your glowing ball rolls straight down the corridor until the paths split. Pick a way, swipe again, and race to the golden stairs.

App Store ↗ Google Play ↗

Three ways to play

  • Journey: 60 levels across five zones, with up to three stars on each: find the way out, beat par, collect every gem.
  • Daily Maze: a new maze every day, the same for everyone. Your best time counts.
  • Endless: mazes that keep growing. Three hearts, and faster runs score more.

Five zones

  • Outer Halls: sunlit sandstone, for learning to run.
  • Catacombs: fog hides the way, and your torch lights only what is near.
  • Vaults: locked doors. Find their keys first.
  • Lair: the Minotaur loves to play tag, and he is quick.
  • Heart: everything at once.

Gems and power-ups

  • Hammer breaks a wall.
  • Map shows the whole maze.
  • Compass lights the way.
  • Smoke makes the Minotaur dizzy.
  • Daily and weekly challenges earn extra gems and badges.

Good to know

  • Free to play and supported by ads. There is nothing to buy.
  • Plays offline, with nothing to sign up for.
  • Leaderboards and achievements are optional, through Game Center on iPhone and iPad and Google Play Games on Android.
  • For players 13 and over.
  • On iPhone and iPad there is more to explore: Switchworks and Portal Sanctum stages, Weekly Expeditions, and rooms to restore in Your Labyrinth.
  • Simple swipe controls, with a one-cell-per-swipe option in Settings.
