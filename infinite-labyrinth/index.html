Infinite Labyrinth

Find the way out. Beat the clock.

Get it on Google Play

The game

Swipe your way through torch-lit stone labyrinths. Collect gems, find the keys to locked doors, and keep clear of the Minotaur.

Three ways to play

  • Journey: 60 levels across five zones, with up to three stars on each.
  • Daily Maze: one maze for everyone each day; your best time counts.
  • Endless: mazes keep growing; beat par for bonus points.

Good to know

  • Rated for everyone (3+). Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • Plays fully offline. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional and adds leaderboards and cloud saves.
