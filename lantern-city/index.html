Lantern City

A rhythm game about one thing: tapping right on the beat. A marker lights up, a performer leaps to it, and you tap the moment it lands. Every catch uncovers a piece of a hidden picture.

App Store ↗ Google Play ↗

How a stage plays

  • A marker appears a moment before the beat, in the shape of the performer on its way.
  • The performer leaps up from below the stage. Tap it as it lands.
  • Right on the beat is Perfect; a touch early or late is Good.
  • Every catch in a row builds your combo, and every ten raises your multiplier.

The story

The night the Great Prism went dark, its hundred and twenty-five glow-notes scattered across twenty-five islands. Pip, a small yellow leaper, sets off with best friend Zest and Glim, the first glow-note found, to catch every one back.

It is told in comic panels between stages. Nobody is wicked, and every headliner joins the parade once beaten.

What is inside

  • Ten performers in 3D, each with its own trick in the air.
  • Twenty-five islands and 121 headliners to out-perform.
  • 125 pictures to uncover, a jigsaw piece at a time.
  • DJ mode: free play on any track you have opened, and a daily track.

Good to know

  • Every island, every difficulty and DJ mode are free, and there is nothing to buy.
  • A short ad can follow a cleared stage, and only a cleared stage: never mid-stage, and never after your first clear.
  • Normal and Hard for every stage. A stage that keeps getting away from you offers a helping hand.
  • Music, sound effects and vibration can each be switched off in Settings.
  • Switches for clearer colours, a calmer stage, bigger performers, and taps in place of swipes and holds.
  • A timing screen measures how late your speaker or headphones are, so the judging stays fair.
